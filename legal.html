Privacy Notice

1. Introduction

What-a-Wash! is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your personal information when using our laundry management services.

2. Essential Purposes

We collect personal information necessary to fulfill essential business purposes, including account creation, laundry order processing, doorstep pick-up and delivery routing, customer notification, and processing payments securely.

3. Secondary Purposes

With your explicit consent, we may process contact details to send promotional announcements, operational service upgrades, customer feedback surveys, and personalized wash reminder notifications.

4. Categories of Data

We collect full names, delivery addresses, contact numbers, email addresses, laundry order histories, garment care preferences, payment confirmations, and digital usage logs generated when navigating our web platform.

5. Methods of Collection

Data is collected directly when you fill out online booking forms, register a laundry account, interact with customer support representatives, or submit inquiry details via our web portal.

6. Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on contractual necessity (delivering laundry services), legal compliance, explicit customer consent, and legitimate operational business interests.

7. Data Subject Rights (RA 10173)

Under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), as a customer ("Data Subject"), you are guaranteed specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your personal and delivery data is processed.
  • Right to Access & Rectification: You can request a copy of your stored laundry profile and demand corrections to inaccurate or out-of-date information.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You may request the suspension, withdrawal, or deletion of your personal data from our laundry management systems upon account termination.
  • Right to Object: You may object to data processing for direct marketing or automated profiling at any time.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your order history and account data in a structured, commonly used electronic format.

8. Compliance Requirements & DPO

To adhere to regulatory requirements mandated by the National Privacy Commission (NPC) under RA 10173:

  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): What-a-Wash! has appointed a Data Protection Officer to oversee compliance, conduct privacy impact assessments, and address customer privacy inquiries. Reach our DPO at dpo@whatawash.com.
  • Breach Notification Protocol: In the event of a security breach involving sensitive personal data, we enforce mandatory notification to affected data subjects and the NPC within 72 hours of verification.

9. Security Measures

What-a-Wash! implements robust technical, organizational, and physical security safeguards to protect customer data:

  • Technical Safeguards: 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all online booking submissions, role-based access control (RBAC), and secure hashed password storage.
  • Organizational Safeguards: Annual data privacy training for laundry operational staff and strict non-disclosure agreements for personnel handling customer address logs.
  • Physical Safeguards: Secure server facilities and restricted physical access to printed laundry dispatch receipts containing customer address details.
